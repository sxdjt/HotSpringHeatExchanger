## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-06

Initial release.

### Added

- `HotSpringHeatExchanger` building: 25 steel, 150 work, gated behind Geothermal Heating research.
  Feeds 250 units into the Dubs Bad Hygiene Sewage pipe network with no fuel and no power.
- `HotSpringTappable` terrain affordance, granted to Odyssey's `HotSpring` terrain by
  `Patches/Patch_HotSpringAffordance.xml`, which restricts placement to hot spring water and
  removes the need for a bridge.
- Custom 128x128 texture at `Textures/sxdjt/HotSpringHeatExchanger.png`.
